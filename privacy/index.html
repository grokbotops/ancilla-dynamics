What this policy covers

This policy describes how Ancilla Dynamics handles personal information for the AI phone front desk demo and its text messages. The current program is a demo that uses fake data. The demo takes messages and callback requests. It does not use calendar booking. When a real person calls, the assistant may collect the information listed below.

Information we collect

The phone system collects only what the call flow is built to collect:

  • The phone number the person called from, and a different callback number if they give one
  • First and last name, if they give it
  • The address where the work is, if they give it
  • The reason for the call, in the caller's words
  • Language used on the call
  • Whether the business has worked with them before, if that question is asked
  • A preferred time for a callback
  • Inside and outside temperature, if those questions are asked
  • Whether someone in the home is at risk, if that question is asked
  • Anything else the caller asks the office to know
  • The date and time of the call
  • A call recording, if the greeting said the call is recorded
  • The caller's yes or no to receiving texts, once that ask is part of the call
  • Text replies the person sends to Ancilla Dynamics
  • For owner notifications, the business owner's phone number, collected during setup

The current demo may also show sample names, addresses, and phone numbers that are not real, such as (000) 000-0000.

How we use this information

We use this information to:

  • Send callback confirmation texts to a caller who said yes
  • Send the business owner a notification about the call, on the owner's own number
  • Help the office call the person back and handle the request
  • Answer HELP requests and honor STOP requests

These texts are not marketing texts.

We use service providers, including the phone and texting platform, to run the front desk and to send texts.

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in consent

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. We do not share this information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Opt-out

A person can reply STOP to any text from this program to opt out. After that, Ancilla Dynamics will not send further texts to that number. Reply HELP for help, or email mendicant-bias@agentmail.to. To opt back in after a STOP, reply START, once that keyword is in service.
