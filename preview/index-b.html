The front desk

Forward your line and the front desk handles the call the way your shop would.

  • Answers and says it's an automated assistant
  • Plays the gas and CO warning first and tells callers to call 911
  • Routes fire, injury, leaks, and burning smells to the safety script
  • Takes the name, callback number, address, and problem, and reads the number back
  • Pages you on urgent calls
  • Sends you a summary of every call

How it works

  1. 01

    Forward your line.

    Send calls to the front desk after hours, when you're on a job, or all day.

  2. 02

    It answers and listens.

    The desk uses AI to understand what the caller says. Every word it says back comes from a script your shop approves.

  3. 03

    Safety comes first.

    Gas, carbon monoxide, fire, and injury calls get the safety script before any questions.

    “If you smell gas, or your carbon monoxide alarm is going off, leave the house now. Call 911 or your gas company.”

  4. 04

    You get the call, written down.

    Urgent calls page you by phone right away. Every other call lands as a summary with the caller's name, number, address, and problem.

Read a full 2:14 AM no-heat call

A real script from our Cedar Bayou example shop.

  1. Front desk

    Thanks for calling Cedar Bayou Air & Heat. This is the automated assistant.

  2. Front desk

    This call is recorded.

  3. Front desk

    If you smell gas, or your carbon monoxide alarm is going off, leave the house now. Call 911 or your gas company. Do not wait for a callback. For anything else, tell me what you need help with, like no heat, no cooling, a water leak, or a tune-up.

  4. Caller

    Our heat's out. It says 58 in here.

  5. Front desk

    Is anyone in the home elderly, a baby, or someone with a health problem that the heat or cold could make worse?

  6. Caller

    Yes, my mother is staying with us.

  7. Front desk

    Okay. I'm going to get this to the right person now. I just need three things.

  8. Front desk

    What's your first and last name?

  9. Caller

    Dana Reyes.

  10. Front desk

    Is the number you're calling from the best one to reach you?

  11. Caller

    Yes.

  12. Front desk

    What's the address where the unit is?

  13. Caller

    It's in Baytown, 77521.

  14. Front desk

    I've sent this to the office as urgent.

  15. Front desk

    They'll call you back as soon as they can.

  16. Front desk

    Keep your phone close so you don't miss the call.

  17. Front desk

    If anything changes, like you smell gas or see smoke, leave the house and call 911.
