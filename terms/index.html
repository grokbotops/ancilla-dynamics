Program name

These terms apply to Ancilla Dynamics Front Desk Alerts, a text program from Ancilla Dynamics LLC.

What messages are sent

This program sends:

  • Callback confirmations to a person who called the Ancilla Dynamics AI phone front desk demo and said yes to texts about that call
  • Owner notifications to the business owner's own number, collected during setup

The current program is a demo that uses fake data. The demo takes messages and callback requests. It does not use calendar booking. Missed-call text-back, Spanish texts, reminder texts, and after-hours texts are out of scope and are not sent. These messages are not marketing.

How you opt in

During the call, after the callback request is taken, the automated assistant asks:

“Can Ancilla Dynamics text you updates about this call from Front Desk Alerts? Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. The privacy policy and terms are on the website. Please say yes or no.”

Texts are sent only after a spoken yes. After a yes, a confirmation text is sent first, then the callback confirmation. If the caller says no, no text is sent to the caller.

Consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, typically one to three messages per call.

Rates

Msg & data rates may apply.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to opt out. After that, Ancilla Dynamics will not send further texts to that number. To opt back in after a STOP, reply START.
