Front Desk Alerts text program

Ancilla Dynamics Front Desk Alerts is a text program from Ancilla Dynamics LLC for its AI phone front desk demo. It sends callback confirmations to a person who called the demo and said yes to texts about that call, and owner notifications to the business owner's own number. Texts go to a caller only after a spoken yes on the call. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency varies, typically one to three messages per call. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
